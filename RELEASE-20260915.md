# GLOBAL OWNER — 20260915.1

## Implemented

New responsive ivory/mint storefront; original branding and product photographs preserved. 17 product-model cards: 5 beauty, 3 lifestyle-device models (Secret Power M/L are options), and 9 Dr. Foothealth insole models.

Seven storefront languages: Korean, English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Indonesian and Hindi. Product title and price/MV labels translate; a localized product poster can be exported as PNG with the original photo.

Search, category filter, product details, known size selection, persistent device-local cart, quantity changes, retail-price subtotal, destination selection and explicitly unsubmitted order-draft TXT export. Member prices are displayed, not automatically granted.

Product-data helper with text input, optional browser microphone permission, speech recognition, speech synthesis, opt-in continuous conversation, stop controls and draggable launcher. This is NOT a connected generative-AI service.

Original index preserved byte-for-byte as research.html. Original app.js, styles.css and assets are untouched. Source baseline: e2a19b7ab2c5e8f983d5b022d90da18eb6ada955.

## Deliberately not represented as complete

Actual payments, seller order submission, live order/delivery lookup, CRM, authenticated membership, generative-AI integration, confirmed shipping/customs/taxes, live currency conversion and full translation of original research/promotion images are NOT implemented. Seven unpriced insole models cannot be added to the cart. Only supplied Medical XL 285–295 mm and Actirun XS 225–235 mm prices/sizes are used. The storefront says these limitations explicitly.

Sales registration, support contacts, delivery and returns terms, payment-provider onboarding and supported destination/product rules require confirmation before selling. No raw card details, identity numbers, customer addresses or certificate images are collected by this release. The business certificate's personal birthdate is not published.

No new promises of disease treatment, height growth or medical efficacy are made. Original research images remain in the separate legacy material page and have not received a new regulatory review.

## Verification

Offline Chromium render using exact local source, scripts, CSS and embedded original image bytes. Navigation is restricted in the execution environment, so browser storage and history were mocked for in-memory tests. Tested all seven languages, 17 cards, seven unpriced models disabled, viewport widths 320/360/390/412/690/768/1440 without horizontal overflow, cart restoration from validated JSON, KRW268000 subtotal (cream x2 and Actirun x1), order-draft Blob creation, translated PNG generation, and safe literal rendering of user chat text. No JavaScript runtime errors occurred in those tests.

Physical microphone/TTS testing and live browser navigation were NOT performed. Browser/device recognition and installed voice availability vary; unsupported and denied-permission states are handled. Deployment success must be verified separately from these local checks.
